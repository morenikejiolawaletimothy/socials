# Logos

The Bluecut Socials primary mark: a circular `primary-600` disc with a white "b" monogram.

- **bluecut-mark.png**: 1024×1024 transparent PNG, ink is `primary-600` (#224cd5) on transparent. Use on light surfaces (`bg-page`, `bg-card`, light tints). Doubles as the app icon and favicon source. Keep clear space of at least half the diameter. Never recolor, outline or distort.

Only the primary blue mark was supplied. Reversed or white and all-ink variants for `bg-dark` and `primary-600` surfaces, plus any horizontal wordmark lockup, are not in the source. Request them from the brand owner before shipping dark-surface placements.
